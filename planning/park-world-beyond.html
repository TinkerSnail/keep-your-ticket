The line through it all is the coast highway, and the commute is a drive down it. Christina's ask on 2026-09-04: a Pacific Coast Highway equivalent, and the reading that if this were Pacifica to Santa Cruz the character would live north of the park and the hills and the tunnel would be there. The park fills the shelf from the water to the range's toe, so a through highway cannot pass it along the shore; it goes behind the first ridge instead, in the trough the range profile leaves at its inner edge, a level saddle about ten metres up with almost no spur relief all the way round. Built the same day, and it is the plan's second alignment. The first looped the second ridge's dip valley 770m out with a crest turnout for a wide view down onto the park, and the generator rejected it: the spurs stand sixty to ninety metres over the valleys between them right down to the foothill, so that loop was seven tunnels totalling 2.7km, and the road could not climb the second ridge at 1:12 fast enough to top it, so the crest was buried.
