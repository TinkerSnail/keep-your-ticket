02B · The world beyond the park

world coordinates · 1.8 × 3.7 km · north up

What this map is. The footprint map's frame stops 240m south of the plaza and cannot hold a pass a kilometre out or a shore two kilometres long, so the world outside the developed envelope is planned here, in the same world metres, at a scale where the park is a small rectangle. Built layers are drawn from ParkPlan's own numbers: both coast outlines and the promontory spine, the crescent range's toe and its named summits and spurs, the approach road as far as it exists, the front road, the parking and the developed envelope. Proposal layers are dashed and exist only on this map until Christina has looked at them and a package builds them.

The line through it all is the coast highway, and the commute is a drive down it. Christina's ask on 2026-09-04: a Pacific Coast Highway equivalent, and the reading that if this were Pacifica to Santa Cruz the character would live north of the park and the hills and the tunnel would be there. The park fills the shelf from the water to the range's toe, so a through highway cannot pass it along the shore; it goes behind the first ridge instead, in the trough the range profile leaves at its inner edge, a level saddle about ten metres up with almost no spur relief all the way round. Built the same day, and it is the plan's second alignment. The first looped the second ridge's dip valley 770m out with a crest turnout for a wide view down onto the park, and the generator rejected it: the spurs stand sixty to ninety metres over the valleys between them right down to the foothill, so that loop was seven tunnels totalling 2.7km, and the road could not climb the second ridge at 1:12 fast enough to top it, so the crest was buried. The drive as built, from the north: the house in a small town on the north coast; the highway on the range's shore-fade face 60m inland, thirty to sixty metres up, forest above and the sea below, with the headland and the lighthouse a speck across the water (Q−3, first sight); two short tunnels where the face is steep; the descent round the arm's foot to 45m inland, where the bay opens with the lighthouse ahead and the tower behind it (Q−2); the trough behind the park, where the park never shows, which is the wait; the junction at the foothill's back, and the park's own road cresting the foothill with the gate, palms, wheel and tower ahead and the parking below (Q−1, the arrival). Far, opening, arrival. What this alignment does not give is a view down onto the park from height: a through road never climbs behind the park, and a high view would be a lookout of its own, not the highway. The same road in reverse is the drive home at close, and the one not taken on the night the player stays. The highway goes on round behind the parking and out to the bay's far shore to the south-east; the park sits on a spur off it, the built approach road, as a beach boardwalk sits off Highway 1.

The headland, as one landform (Trinidad Head proportions). Built 2026-09-04. The lighthouse district's 4m pad and the promontory were two things where the reference has one. Built: the north outline widened into a headland about 240m long and up to 140m wide with the point at (−242, −295); the spine up its middle as one curve with no bend over sixteen degrees; a level top at 30m near the point, not the forty first proposed, because the walk climbs the spine from the pad at 1:7.7 and forty would have been 1:5.5; the crown set by distance to the shoreline with a 20m cliff skirt, dropping to a low floor at the two coves on the north-west face, which the outline's cliff band cuts as before; the feature bounded narrowly through the turn off the pad and widely past it, so it lifts nothing under the park; the lighthouse and the keeper's exhibit on the level top 37m from the tip, about 27m inside the cliffs on both faces, with a forest clearing round them; the walk re-laid up the spine to the forecourt's rim, walked by the controller and standing on its ground; and in the cove's lee a short breakwater with a light at its head, a plank quay with bollards, and two boats. The lighthouse's frames and the drive's Q−2 and Q−3 were re-shot. What is not built: the path down to one cove, which needs 1:8 over a 25m drop on the cliff face and is a walk test of its own.

South of the park. Soft, long and mostly silhouette, since only the pier head, the wheel, the sky ride, the head of the east climb and the aerials look at it. A low beach past the Fairground and the parking, with Coastal Creek's outfall where package 05A puts it at the Boardwalk's south end; the parking clause's 20m bluff and cove; then a long dune-backed beach curving south-east under the hills the south arm already brings to the shore, with one or two sea stacks off it outside the sunset sector; and a low forested far headland closing the bay's south-east end as a silhouette, with the distant city's skyline beyond it by day and its glow by night. One rocky end per bay: the north has it, so the south gets no cliffs along its length.

The tunnels and the towns. Tunnels are derived, not placed: a stretch buried deeper than 9m for more than 40m is bored, lined, given a headwall at each portal and a lamp every 24m; everything shallower is a cutting with an 18m batter. On this alignment that is two on the north coast, 152m and 122m, the second at the arm's foot facing the headland, and two on the far shore, 98m and 157m. The rejected loop's crest turnout, junction tunnel and hill town in the dip are gone from the map; a hill town would now sit off the north coast road or beyond the trough, and is not drawn until the drive says where. The north town with the house, the beach town on the shelf, and the distant city beyond the far headland stand as proposals. The towns are scenery, not sections: driven through and looked at, never walked in this milestone. The trough passes about a hundred metres behind the parking's front road and the arrival walk; the parking clause's berms and hedge are what stand between them, and whether the highway shows from the lots is a frame to shoot.

Frames. Three join Q0 to Q6 in package 06, shot on 2026-09-04 from the car's eye and kept in documentation/screenshots/highway-02B-2026-09-04/: Q−3, high on the north coast looking at the lighthouse across the water; Q−2, the view bend on the descent looking at the tower past the headland; and Q−1, the park's road cresting the foothill looking at the tower. All three are drawn as cones from the car's eye. The glimpse bends are marked as the sequence they make; a glimpse is a moving thing and gets a frame sequence rather than a still.
