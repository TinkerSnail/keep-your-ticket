Beach Town

The shore road becomes the public promenade; shops, beach services, pier and skate park are changes to its existing blocks and dead-end lot.

Literal-use rule. Activity routes intentionally follow public edges; they are not new landscaping. No SVG line or coordinate may be traced into generated geometry. Roads, shore, promenade, pier and rail require named editor-owned sources before construction.

Public edge

The promenade replaces the seaward curb condition. Dunes, three beach paths, rinse posts, lifeguard station and bins terminate directly on that edge.

Commercial row

Surf school, surf shop and skate shop retrofit existing shop bays between cross streets. Seafood occupies existing waterfront blocks; shacks and waste face the service street.

Skate park

The shallow bowl, bank, quarter-pipe, ledge and sitting edge replace part of the south dead-end lot. They do not occupy sand or arrive as a slab over the lot.

Town movement

Crossings connect shops, promenade and beach access. Skater and surfer loops use those routes; the station meets the same pedestrian network.
