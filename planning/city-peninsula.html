City Peninsula

Waterfront, regional terminal, working port, downtown core, hillside neighborhoods and skyline anchors. The facelift replaces the undifferentiated massing; it does not add towers over it.

Waterfront hierarchy

A continuous public/working edge replaces the old first row. Port quays occupy one end, the rail terminal joins the center, and the downtown core begins behind them.

Block replacement

Selected existing massing blocks become the terminal, port sheds and two or three skyline anchors. Nothing is stacked through an intact old block.

Hillside city

Neighborhood bands follow the two existing hills and their contours. The grid bends and thins as it climbs instead of continuing as a flat downtown carpet.

Distance composition

Waterfront band, anchor spacing and hill silhouettes are judged from park, pier, highway and aerial frames. Detail remains distance-painted while the 400m player far plane stands.
