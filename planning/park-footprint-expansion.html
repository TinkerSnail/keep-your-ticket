02A · Shape brief — the range before its trees

Summits, three and unequal. Saddleback, the high one: bearing −20°, about 1,000m from the crescent's centre, 330m, bare rock above 260. The Lookout, north: bearing −100°, about 900m, 260m, wooded to the top, the dome the lighthouse crown reads against. Kiln Hill, south-east: bearing +55°, about 950m, 240m. Saddles between them at 150 to 190m. From the Plaza's east axis the summit line stands behind the Terraced Fountain and the coaster is still held right.

Spurs run down toward the park, not rings round it. Five ridgelines from the summit line to the toe, on bearings −135° (dying into the sea as the north headland's cliffs), −70° (behind the Grove and the north rides), −10° (the east spur, becoming the foothill behind the High Terrace where the plateau hands over), +40° (behind Kiddieland) and +95° (the south spur, ending behind the parking). Valleys between them, 40 to 80m below the adjacent ridges at mid-slope; the road valley on bearing +75° is the widest.

The approach road. Arrives from the south-east down the +75° valley, swings west below the toe of the south spur, and meets the arrival axis south of the parking fields at about z 240. It is a landform decision first: the valley floor stays under 1:12 and nothing of the crescent's south arm crosses it. The parking clause below fixes the junction, the lot road, the edges and the arrival view.

The coast, after Half Moon Bay. A headland juts out at the north end of a crescent bay, a harbour cove sits in its lee, and the shore runs on south in one concave arc. Here: the working shore either side of the Boardwalk, z ±70, protected as built; north of it a cove biting in between the wooden coaster and the lighthouse's headland to (−72, −218), twelve metres seaward of the Grand Circuit's lane; the promontory running west from that headland's root to a cliffed point at (−218, −262), the land between the cove's north shore and the coast beyond; north of the point a cliffed coast with two coves between z −300 and −420; south of the Boardwalk the bay's far shore, holding about x −125 past the parking's front road with the 20m bluff and its cove on that stretch, then sweeping south-east across the mainland reserve's west edge near z 530 and on to (700, 2200). Reshaped 2026-09-04: the first outlines ran both arms out to sea at about sixteen degrees, a V with the park at the tip of a cape; Half Moon Bay is a coast trending north-west to south-east with the point standing out of it and the harbour in its lee, so the coast north of the point now recedes north-north-west and stays east of the point's x for 400m, and the south shore recedes into the hills instead of out to sea. The reserve carries the far shore where the outline has crossed its west edge, clipped to the same outline, over a second sheet of water; the transition ramps and bench boxes that had filled the cove north of the coaster and hung over the water are retired, and the coast meshes are the ground beyond the working strip. Steepness and roughness. The foothill front stays about 1:2.75; the second ridge moves in to 280m and the summit ridge to 900m so the climb is 1:3 rather than 1:5. Judged from six standpoints, not from the sea: the pier head, the promenade's north end, the lighthouse forecourt, the head of the east climb, the belvedere, and the parking arrival looking both ways. Those frames are the acceptance; the sea and plan views are diagnostics.

02A · Planting clause — the forest on the range

Where. Forest wherever the ground has risen at least 15m above its meadow and stands below the 260m treeline; sparse clumps on the meadow shoulder between the park and the toe; nothing inside the developed envelope, which keeps its own props; nothing within 9m of the approach road, on any path, or below the shoreline. Trees stand on terrain only.

What. Two shapes, conifer and broadleaf, mixed roughly 62/38 in the forest and 35/65 on the meadow, 8 to 16m tall, in the forest band's own green varied ±15% per tree. They are instanced meshes of about fifty triangles placed at runtime from a seeded jittered grid resolved onto the ground, so the forest needs no scene and can be re-tuned without regenerating the park.

How many, measured. 110 trees per hectare on the forest band within 560m of the crescent's centre as full meshes, 200 per hectare beyond it as two-triangle impostor cards so the distant canopy closes, 3 on the meadow: about 175,000 trees. Tree shadows are off: with them the forest cost about 0.2ms per thousand meshes and 45/ha ran 5ms over the vsync floor; without them, and with the far tier as cards, the whole forest adds nothing measurable at the promenade or the climb head and costs about 1.5s of planting at startup. The measurements live in the 2026-09-04 journal; a change of density is re-measured, not guessed.

Palms (built 2026-09-04). Thirty-four generated palms, a struck trunk and seven frond slabs each: rows on both edges of the arrival walk at x ±11.5 every 12m from the drop-off to the gate, the front road's south verge every 13m with a gap at the drop-off, and eight along the Boardwalk deck's seaward edge clear of the pier root and the alley mouth. Nothing here touches NT-1 or NT-2, which take no planting from this layer.
