South Shore Plan

The land edit 2 B added to the coast south of the beach town, drawn as a Cayucos-style neighbourhood (Christina: “this is cayucos, great example for houses and layout”; “we expanded the land into the ocean westward so we could have more residents on the coast”). Beach Road carries on from the town's main drag, comes down the bluff and runs the length of the bench, with about 220 small houses close-set on both sides, a few shops, the colony's condos and beach motel where people arrive, a way to the beach every 110 to 150 m, and a second way out at the south end. Drawn over the terrain master as it stood on 2 October 2026; world metres, x east, z south, north up. A is approved, P proposed, E exists: here the bench, the beach and the bluff exist only in the terrain master, authored but not yet in the game. The promise

What the South Shore is for

Where the coast's people live: the beach town's cooks and lifeguards, the park's staff, retired couples, and the summer people in the rentals. A long street under the bluff with the sea just past the houses: surfers' cars at the beach lanes at dawn, garage doors open on Saturday, kids biking the path after school, the train going by at the bottom of the gardens, porch lights at dusk. Nobody comes here for a ride; they come home. It is worth coming back to because the street changes by the hour and the season, and no two houses are alike.

Zone by zone

Where everything goes

AThe way down

  • Beach Road carries on from the town's main drag (beach town plan, pass 12) past the skate park and the new lot. At J1, the lot's south end, the branch up pass 9's line keeps left and Beach Road goes on down the bluff's face to the bench: 99 m from 3.2 to −4.1 m Godot, about 7.3 %, in a cut of up to 2 m on its land side. Since the beach town plan's pass 15 the branch no longer climbs to the highway: it turns at J3 on a 44 m bend and goes under the highway into the hillside (Christina: “why cant we put the crossing of beach road under the highway”, then “a”). For 39 m below J1 the ramp and the branch share an edge, one falling as the other rises, so a wall stands between them, up to 6.0 m (pass 2).
  • It needs the ground of pass 9's 13 turnouts; they move down to the canal green (decision 1).
  • Pass 9's trail joins the path down the ramp. Its last stretch stays as the bluff walk, level along the old shelf, to the lookout, which stays where it was drawn. Pass 1 laid the walk on the branch's line between z 585 and 615; pass 2 moves it off: from the ramp's path at z 603 it crosses the ramp and climbs about 3.6 m at about 11 % along the wall's foot to the shelf at (9.7, 629.6), then runs on its old line.
  • Contract: inhabitable.

BThe north end

  • Where people arrive: the ramp's foot, the canal green and two shops. The green is the canal outlet's corridor (z 668) kept clear, 24 m wide from the bluff to the beach. Until a canal is chosen it is a lawn with the main beach access, a comfort station and a gravel lot for the 14 cars from the turnouts.
  • The colony moves here (decision 5): the beach motel at the ramp's foot, 32 m of two-storey rooms looking over the track to the sea; three condo blocks on the sea side, the chunky repeating 1970s kind of three storeys, and one on the land side, two storeys of flat-roofed stucco (Christina, 2 October: “one of the rows should be like that chunky repeating lowrises in the top left of this pic, others should be like the ones in the forground”, of her photo reference/houses/beach_lot_aerial.webp).
  • Two neighbourhood shop buildings, after her second photo: a market and deli in brown siding under a red metal roof, with a deck and a pole sign over its 8-car lot; and a plain white two-storey block, a café and a beach rental below two flats.
  • Contract: the green and the street inhabitable; the shops observable (real doors and windows, to open later); motel and condos enclosing.

CThe north rows

  • From the canal green to where the track leaves the bench, about 870 m. Here the track runs between the houses and the beach, 10 m in from the crest, so the sea-side gardens end at its fence.
  • Two rows where the bench allows. From about z 800 to 1030 it is pinched, 42 to 50 m from the bluff's foot to the track, too little for two rows: Beach Road runs along the foot with one row of houses on its sea side.
  • Beach accesses N1 to N5 every 140 m, plus the canal and creek greens: 4 m paths over the track with bells and swing gates, a bin at each end and a rinse-off post at the crest. No car crosses the track here (see “Where the brief did not fit”).
  • Contract: street, path and accesses inhabitable; houses enclosing; the track observable.

DThe crossing

  • From the departure at (295, 1468) the track crosses the bench on the slant, climbing at 2 %, to the foot of the bluff. Beach Road meets it once, at about (353, 1514), where the track is about 2.2 m over the bench: the street rises at 5 % for about 45 m each way to a gated level crossing (decision 2).
  • No houses where the track's bank crosses; the bank is planted with ice plant and coastal scrub.
  • Contract: inhabitable.

EThe beach rows

  • South of the crossing the track is up on the bluff's face on its own shelf, so the sea-side houses back straight onto the beach: their lots stop 2 m short of the crest, and nothing is built on the sand.
  • The land-side houses back onto the foot of the track's bank; trains pass above their back fences.
  • Accesses S1 to S4 every 130 m: two paths, and two lanes with eight stalls each at the crest, where the surfers park; the inlet green has six more.
  • Contract: as C.

FThe south end

  • The bench narrows to nothing by z 2240. Beach Road turns up the south link (decision 3): 111 m at about 4 %, under the rail viaduct with about 14 m clear, to the highway's southbound lanes where the highway is lowest, about 0.5 m Godot; right in and right out, like the town's streets.
  • The path ends at the south lookout on the bench's tip, over the beach to the viaduct, the inlet and the bridge to the city; a turnaround and eight stalls.
  • Contract: inhabitable; the viaduct observable.

·Beach Road on the bench

  • 15 m between the lot lines: a 1.5 m sidewalk and a 2.2 m parking lane on the land side; a 6 m carriageway, the town's width; a 1.3 m strip for the palms, the wooden power poles and the lamps; and the 4 m two-way hike-and-bike path on the sea side, carried the whole way from the town to the south lookout, as the coastal brief asks (“a hike and bike sidewalk on the sea side”).
  • 1629 m on the bench, flat at −4.1 to −4.4 m Godot (3.1 to 3.4 m over the sea), plus the 99 m ramp and the 111 m south link. One street the length of the coast, from the town to the spur.

·The houses

  • Cayucos's way: small lots, 1 to 2 m between houses, garages and driveways to the street, small yards and little lawn, single tall fan palms and dark windswept cypress. Three families, shuffled so that no family comes three times running:
  • A · stucco under clay tile: two storeys in cream, pink, yellow or peach, hipped or gabled, the garage to the street; 11 m lots. A new kit piece.
  • B · gables in shingle or painted siding: the kit's court cottage (6 × 8 m, one storey) on 9 m lots with a driveway to a carport, or its hillside cottage (8 × 7 m) over a garage storey on 10 m lots.
  • C · flat-roofed stucco boxes: two storeys, a roof deck with a railing, the garage in the ground floor; 9 m lots. A new kit piece.
  • Lots 14.5 to 30 m deep, as the bench allows (median 18 m); houses 4 to 5.5 m back from the sidewalk for the driveway. Contract: enclosing (public faces, porches, garage doors, lit windows; no interiors).

Decisions

Five for Christina

1 · Where Beach Road comes down

Recommended: at J1, the new lot's south end, straight down the bluff's face to the bench, 99 m at about 7.3 %. It takes the ground of pass 9's 13 turnouts, which move down to the canal green. Or: leave from the foot of the climb at z 605, keeping the turnouts; the ramp then lands inside the canal outlet's corridor, so the outlet would have to move about 50 m south.

2 · Crossing the track

Recommended: one gated level crossing where the track leaves the bench, Beach Road rising 2.2 m to it at 5 %: one street the length of the coast. Or: no road crossing. Beach Road stops either side of the track, walkers and bikes cross on a gated path, and the south half depends on the south link alone.

3 · The south end

Recommended: the south link under the viaduct to the highway's southbound lanes, right in and right out: a second way in for fire and ambulance, and a way home without turning round. Or: the same link gated for emergencies only, open to walkers and bikes; or no link at all, a 1.7 km dead end serving about 240 homes, which I would not build.

4 · How close the houses are

Recommended: Cayucos-close, two rows wherever the bench is wide enough (about 47 m of usable ground between the bluff's foot and the track's fence or the crest): about 220 houses on 9 to 11 m lots. Or: one row of deeper lots on the sea side only, about 100 houses, each with a real garden: quieter, and half the dressing.

5 · The colony and the shops

Recommended: the colony's beach motel and four condo blocks at the ramp's foot beside the canal green, landward of the track, with two neighbourhood shop buildings facing the green. Edit 2 B replaced pass 10's push-out, so the colony's own lane and level crossing go. Or: keep the colony where pass 10 drew it. The ramp, the motel's spot and the canal corridor all overlap it, and pass 10's southmost block stands in the canal corridor.

Measured against the brief

Where the brief did not fit the ground

  • Beach Road cannot run on level. The bench is 7.3 m below Beach Road at the new lot, so the street comes down a ramp, and the ramp needs the turnouts' ground.
  • No lanes to the beach across the track. North of the departure the track runs 10 m in from the crest, which leaves 6 to 10 m of land beyond it: nowhere for a car to end, turn or park. The accesses there are 4 m paths with swing gates; the lifeguard truck and the beach rake pass a bollard. Lanes with stalls work south of the crossing (S2, S4).
  • Nothing to walk down. The bench is 3.5 m over the sea and the beach falls 3 m over 30 m, so the accesses run out level to the crest and onto the sand; no stairs.
  • Not two rows everywhere. From about z 800 to 1030 the bench is only 42 to 50 m from the bluff's foot to the track: one row. Lots are 14.5 to 30 m deep, shallower than the deep lots in her photos.
  • One crossing nobody asked for. The track leaves the bench on the slant, so Beach Road must cross it once, on a 2.2 m hump.
  • The colony moves. Edit 2 B built the bench at 3.5 m over the sea where pass 10 drew a push-out at about 8 m, and the canal corridor runs through pass 10's southmost block.

The questions every proposal answers

Answered for the South Shore

  • Promise. For the people who live on the coast and the summer renters; for the photographer, the street under the bluff with the sea at its end, the train at the bottom of the gardens, the roofs seen from the highway, and the sun setting over the bay from the beach.
  • Why here. It is the only new flat land on the coast: the bench edit 2 B built, 3.5 m over the sea and 51 to 86 m deep between the bluff and the beach crest. The highway runs 17 to 42 m above it, out of the way.
  • Arrive, dwell, leave. By Beach Road from the town and down the ramp; from the highway by the south link, or through the beach town by Shore Street; from the hillside by the branch under the highway to J1 (beach town plan, pass 15); on foot or bike by the path. The nearest train stop is the beach town's station. People dwell on the canal green, the beach, the lookouts and their front steps, none of them in the through-route.
  • Step-free and emergency. The bench is level; the ramp at 7.3 % is the steep part (its sidewalk follows the street), and the south link is about 4 %. Two ways in for fire and ambulance (decision 3); every house is on Beach Road.
  • Staff, supply, close. The market and the café take deliveries from the street at dawn; bins go out to the kerb once a week; the motel's laundry van; the beach rake and the lifeguard truck through the access bollards. The street lights come on at dusk.
  • Water, waste, rear faces. The bench falls 1 % to the crest; storm water runs out under the accesses to the beach. The three water options (canal, creek, inlet) keep their corridors as greens. Power on poles, as in the photos. The rear faces are the gardens: to the bluff, to the track's fence, or, south of the crossing, to the beach, where the backs are the faces: decks, outdoor showers, boards against the fence.
  • What the camera gets. From the highway, a roofscape of tile, shingle and decks over the street to the sea; this place is seen most from above, so the roofs are its face. Down the street, palms and poles in perspective under the bluff. From the beach, the houses' sea faces and the train passing. From the lookouts, up the coast to the town and down it to the viaduct and the bridge.
  • Ownership. Every line here is a review diagram. Beach Road's course, the ramp and the link extend the editor-owned Beach Road curve (approach_roads_layout.tscn), as the beach town plan says of its run south; the land is the terrain master's edit 2; the houses come from the cottage kit (assets/source/props/beach_cottage.blend) plus two new pieces; every lot and building needs its own source. No protected anchor is near: the plaza is about 550 m north of J1.
  • Hour, season, after close. Surfers at the beach lanes at dawn; dog walkers; the train through the day; kids on bikes after school; porch lights and televisions at dusk. Summer: every rental full, towels on the decks. Winter: shutters on the rentals, storm surf up to the crest.
  • Contract. Beach Road, its path, the ramp, the link, the greens, the accesses, the beach and the lookouts inhabitable; the shops observable through real doors and windows, to open later; houses, condos and the motel enclosing; the bluff's face enclosing; the hills above scenic.
  • The next move. The three greens keep the canal, the creek and the inlet open. The land-side lots have room for back cottages. The bluff top by the highway stays open for a stair from a bus stop later. The south link leaves room for a path on toward the bridge.

How this was drawn

The ground. The terrain master, assets/source/world_terrain_master.blend, as saved on 2 October 2026 (sha1 88336c20, after edits 1 to 5), copied to a scratch folder and read there with headless Blender; the master itself was only copied, never opened or written. The ground was sampled on a 2.5 m grid over x −200 to 900, z 380 to 2320 by casting rays at the terrain mesh, which is a 10 m lattice along this coast. The relief tint and shading are drawn from those samples; the contours are drawn from them after a light smoothing. The bluff's foot is measured every 5 m as the −3 m Godot line; its top is where the ground first falls steeper than 1 in 4 below the highway's edge. The beach crest and the shore before edit 2 come from the edit's design file (documentation/terrain/renders/applied_coast_design.json), the waterline from the master's coast_extension_B_shoreline. The readout's heights come from the same samples, every 5 m; the surroundings are sampled every 10 m over x −1500 to 2200, z −200 to 2900, for context only. Heights are Godot metres; the sea is at −7.5, so “over the sea” is Godot + 7.5.

Measured: the bench 51 to 86 m deep between the foot and the crest, at −4.0 to −4.6 m Godot; the foot at 55 to 65 m from the highway's centreline; the highway's ground 13 to 38 m Godot along the bench, falling to about 0.5 m at the spur; the rail's lines and heights (the master's rail_south_beach_front_centreline, rail_bluff_shelf_centreline and PROPOSAL_rail_viaduct_spur); the three water markers; every building's ground (all on the bench, −4.6 to −4.0 m) and its distance from the track (6 m or more from the centre line). The highway is straight within 1.2 m from z 600 to 2150 (bearing 156°), so lots are laid out square to it.

Estimated: the ramp's and the link's grades (straight lines over the measured ground, not designed profiles); the road heights (the master drapes the highway's line on the ground; the road's own grade is the generator's); house, shop and condo footprints; unit, room and parking counts. From other plans: the beach town's items from the Beach Town Plan, pass 11 (its own coordinates); the canal outlet from the master's marker, moved to z 668 on 1 October. Not done: no camera views, no Godot run, nothing written to the master, ParkPlan or the game.

Sunset. This land lies 154° to 178° from the pier head and the promenade, at least 102° clear of the protected 280° to 310° water (edit 2's report). From the beach the 294° sunset bearing runs out over the bay (checked against the master's coast and city outlines).
